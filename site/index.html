How many curtain panels do I need?

Convert curtain pole or track width, heading fullness ratio, and single-panel flat width into a whole-panel order count—and inspect the actual fullness after rounding.

Quick answer: US window: a 72 in rod at 2× fullness needs 144 in flat width; with 50 in panels in even pairs, order 4 panels (200 in flat width, 2.78× fullness). Metric: a 180 cm track at 2× fullness needs 360 cm flat width; buying even pairs of 140 cm panels requires 4 panels (3.11× actual fullness).

How the calculation works

Multiply the full curtain rod or track width by your desired fullness multiplier to get the target combined flat width. Divide by the flat finished width of one panel and round up. When buying symmetrical pairs, increment any odd result by one panel, then recompute actual fullness.

What this estimate does not cover

Measure the full rod between finials or full track length, not just window glass. Blackout wraparound returns, center overlap, extra-thick thermal linings, and finished drop length are separate measurements.
