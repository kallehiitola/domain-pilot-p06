How many curtain panels do I need?

Turn curtain track width, desired fullness and finished panel width into a whole-panel purchase count. See the actual fullness after rounding instead of assuming the target is exact.

Quick answer: A 180 cm track at 2× fullness needs 360 cm flat width. Four 140 cm panels are required when purchasing even pairs.

How the calculation works

Multiply track or pole width by desired fullness to find the target combined flat width. Divide by finished panel width and round up. If buying equal pairs, round an odd panel count up to the next even count. Actual fullness is purchased flat width divided by track width.

Worked examples

Example 1

A 180 cm track at 2× fullness needs 360 cm flat width. Four 140 cm panels are required when purchasing even pairs.

Example 2

If individual panels are allowed, 3 panels supply 420 cm and give about 2.33× fullness.

What this estimate does not cover

Measure the track or pole, not just the glass. The appropriate fullness depends on heading style and fabric; check the supplier’s guidance. Panel width here is flat finished width. This does not calculate drop, hems or fabric yardage.
